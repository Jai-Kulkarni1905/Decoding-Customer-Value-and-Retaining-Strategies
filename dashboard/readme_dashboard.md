## Overview

This dashboard translates customer analytics findings into actionable business insights for a D2C fashion brand. It combines customer segmentation, loyalty analysis, promotional behavior, geographic opportunities, and category-level insights to support retention and growth decisions.

## Key Metrics

* Total Customers
* Average Spend
* Promo Dependency Rate
* Loyal Customer Percentage
* High Value Customer Percentage

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Customer Distribution

Visualizes the distribution of customers across value tiers and loyalty status.

### 2. Promo Dependency vs Loyalty

Compares promotional dependency and loyalty rates across customer value tiers to assess whether discounts are driving retention.

### 3. Geographic Opportunity

Identifies regions with strong spending behavior and lower promotional dependence, indicating genuine brand demand.

### 4. Category Funnel

Highlights category-level patterns associated with customer acquisition and retention behavior.

## Business Questions Addressed

* Which customers generate the highest long-term value?
* Are promotions creating loyalty or merely subsidizing purchases?
* Which customer segments should receive retention investments?
* Which regions demonstrate organic growth potential?
* Which product categories contribute most to retention?

## Project Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to help business stakeholders understand customer value distribution, promotional effectiveness, retention opportunities, and strategic growth levers through a single interactive interface.
